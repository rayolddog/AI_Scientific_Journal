### Category I — Directly Fixable Criticisms (Resolved by Calculation & Code in Stage 02)

1. **The $O(\epsilon^2)$ Equalizing Drift in Linear Unitary Beam-Splitter Mixing (Reviewer C Major #1):**  
   *The Critique:* Reviewer C proved that in the exact linear unitary mixing map $a_i' = a_i\cos\epsilon + a_j e^{i\xi_{ij}}\sin\epsilon$, the exact intensity change is
   $$s_i' - s_i = (s_j - s_i)\sin^2\epsilon + \sqrt{s_i s_j}\sin(2\epsilon)\cos\Delta\phi_{ij}.$$
   Under diffusive scaling $2\epsilon^2/\delta t \to \sigma^2$, the second-order term $(s_j - s_i)\sin^2\epsilon$ survives at $O(\epsilon^2/\delta t)$, producing an equalizing drift $\frac{\sigma^2}{2}(s_j - s_i)\,dt$ that pulls shares toward $1/N$ and repopulates empty sites ($s_i = 0 \implies s_i' = s_j\sin^2\epsilon > 0$), destroying boundary absorption!  
   *The Fix (Stage 02 §2.2 & §3.1):* We accept this algebraic proof in full and include it verbatim in §2.2 of the revised manuscript. We show why a *linear* unitary beam-splitter map does **not** generate a pure martingale on the intensities, and we state the two exact mathematical routes that eliminate the $(s_j - s_i)\sin^2\epsilon$ drift:
   - **Route A (Direct Intensity-Simplex Cross-Term Transfer / Itô Martingale Postulate):** Formulating Postulate 2 directly as a pure antisymmetric intensity transfer $\Delta s_i^{(ij)} = 2\epsilon\sqrt{s_i s_j}\cos\Delta\phi_{ij}$ on the energy simplex (or subtracting the diagonal quadratic recoil counter-term $-(s_j - s_i)\sin^2\epsilon$), which corresponds to an amplitude-modulated mixing angle $\sin\theta_{ij} = \epsilon(s_i s_j)^{1/4}$ that vanishes when either site is empty ($s_i = 0$ or $s_j = 0$).
   - **Route B (Nonlinear Kerr/Cross-Saturation Counter-Drift):** Supplying a contact cross-saturation drift $+\frac{\sigma^2}{2}(s_i - s_j)\,dt$ that cancels the linear radiative dispersion $-\frac{\sigma^2}{2}(s_i - s_j)\,dt$ at $O(\epsilon^2)$.

2. **Exact Multivariate Mean Fixation-Time Formula — Kimura–Ohta (1969) vs. 2-Site Shannon Entropy (Reviewer C Major #3, Reviewer B Minor #3):**  
   *The Critique:* Reviewer C proved that applying the neutral Wright–Fisher generator $L = \frac{\sigma^2}{2}\sum_{i,j} s_i(\delta_{ij} - s_j)\partial_i\partial_j$ to the Shannon entropy $H(\mathbf{s}) = -\sum_{i=1}^N s_i\ln s_i$ gives $LH = -\frac{\sigma^2}{2}(k - 1)$ on a face with $k$ surviving sites, which equals $-\frac{\sigma^2}{2}$ *only* when $k = N = 2$.  
   *The Fix (Stage 02 §3.1 & [`code/run_stage02_verification.py`](code/run_stage02_verification.py)):* We replace the 2-site Shannon formula with the exact **Kimura–Ohta (1969)** $N$-allele fixation-time solution of the backward equation $L\mathbb{E}[\tau_{\text{game}}] = -1$:
   $$\mathbb{E}[\tau_{\text{game}}(\mathbf{s}_0)] = -\frac{2}{c_N \sigma^2}\sum_{i=1}^N (1 - s_i(0))\ln(1 - s_i(0)),$$
   where $c_N = \frac{2\lfloor N/2\rfloor}{N(N-1)}$ is the per-step pair activation fraction. Indeed, applying $\sum_{i,j} s_i(\delta_{ij} - s_j)\partial_i\partial_j$ to $f_m(\mathbf{s}) = (1 - s_m)\ln(1 - s_m)$ yields $+s_m$, and summing over $m = 1,\dots,N$ yields $\sum_{m=1}^N s_m = 1$ identically on every $k$-dimensional face of the simplex! In `code/run_stage02_verification.py`, we verify this exact identity across $N = 2, 4, 10$.

We also distinguish literal local mechanical energy shares $s_i(0)$ (our semiclassical ontology) from standard quantum expectation values $\langle\psi|\hat{n}_i|\psi\rangle$ in $|1\rangle = \sum_i c_i |e_i\rangle$ (Reviewer C Major #5).

### Category II — Fundamental Limitations of the Approach (Conceded & Recorded Plainly in Stage 02 §6)

As the Accountable Sponsor noted prior to Stage 02, several criticisms uncovered by the panel reflect **intrinsic structural limitations of a local semiclassical detector-simplex model** that cannot be fixed by algebra or code tweaks:

1. **Spacelike Nonlocality of the Conserved One-Quantum Simplex & Entangled Phase Pinning (Reviewer A Major #1 & #4, Reviewer B Major #4, Reviewer C Major #9):**  
   When candidate absorber sites lie in opposite output arms of a macroscopic beam splitter ($L > c\,\tau_{\text{game}}$) or in a spacelike-separated Bell experiment with equal arm lengths ($L_A = L_B$), retarded luminal QED radiation ($1/r$ dipole fields) **cannot** maintain $\sum_i s_i(t) = 1$ or transmit a phase lock from Station $A$ to Station $B$ before Station $B$ captures its wave packet. Maintaining single-quantum conservation across distant ports and correlating simultaneous Stage-2 walks at $L_A = L_B$ requires an **explicitly nonlocal shared wave ledger** (requiring either a preferred foliation or an operational nonlocal state-space coupling, in the same foundational class as Bohmian mechanics or Pearle/Adler state-space martingales). In Stage 02 (§2, §5, & §6), we replace the broken sequential Stage-1 story ("whichever finishes first pins the other's Stage-1 Malus split") with an explicit simultaneous-arrival ($L_A = L_B$) coupled Stage-2 martingale prescription and state plainly that it is an **assumed nonlocal coupling**, not a local causal derivation of Bell correlations.

2. **Lack of a Standard QED Mechanism for $\Delta_{\text{off}}(s_i) = \omega(1 - s_i)$ and for the In-Phase Vacuum Pump $+2\epsilon_{\text{vac}}\sqrt{s_i}$ (Reviewer A Major #2 & #3, Reviewer B Major #2 & #3, Reviewer C Major #4 & #6):**  
   - Neither a linear harmonic oscillator (Lamb & Scully 1969) nor a textbook two-level atom in the optical Bloch equations has an excitation-dependent transition frequency $\omega_{\text{eff}}(s_i) = s_i\omega$; obtaining $\dot{\varphi}_i = \omega(1 - s_i)$ requires postulating a maximally anharmonic action-angle / Kerr oscillator $H(I_i) = \omega I_i - \frac{\omega}{2\hbar}I_i^2$. Moreover, as Reviewer B noted, the neutral Wright–Fisher martingale already has zero deterministic rich-get-richer drift whenever local dephasing exceeds radiative coupling ($\Gamma_{\text{deph}} \gg \Gamma_{\text{rad}}$), so Theorem 2 is not needed to protect Theorem 1. In Stage 02 (§3.2), we demote Theorem 2 to an optional nonlinear Kerr-oscillator hypothesis.
   - Likewise, the QED vacuum is phase-invariant ($\langle\hat{b}_{\text{vac}}\rangle = 0$) and radiative self-reaction scales as $a_i = \sqrt{s_i}e^{i\phi_i}$ (giving a Lamb shift $\propto s_i$ and spontaneous emission $\propto -s_i$, not an energy pump $+2\epsilon_{\text{vac}}\sqrt{s_i}$). Thus $\tilde{\epsilon}_{\text{vac}}$ cannot be claimed as a first-principles QED effect; it is retained strictly as an empirical/phenomenological drift hypothesis whose inability to cancel threshold hazards across arbitrary $(\theta, \lambda, N)$ is proven analytically in Proposition 1.

3. **Edge-Cycle Constraints in Multi-Site Phase Noise (Reviewer C Major #2):**  
   Relative site phases $\Delta\phi_{ij} = \phi_i - \phi_j$ around any 3-site loop obey $\Delta\phi_{ij} + \Delta\phi_{jk} + \Delta\phi_{ki} = 0$, so independent edge noises $dW_{ij}$ require edge-specific channel fluctuations $\xi_{ij}(t)$ (such as independent random-matching scattering channels or bath modes per pair) in addition to rapid site dephasing $\tau_{\text{deph}} \ll \delta t$. This requirement is now stated explicitly in Postulate 3 and §3.1.
