\begin{abstract}
\noindent Working within a two-spinor Complexified 4D Vacuum ($\mathbb{C}^4 = \mathbb{C}^2_L \oplus \mathbb{C}^2_R$)~\cite{Penrose1967,PenroseRindler1984}, we formulate a self-contained cosmological toy model in which the \textbf{Big Bang is not a $3+1\text{D}$ geometric point singularity of infinite density, but a localized, energy-triggered phase transition (condensate bubble nucleation) inside an infinite, subcritical, scale-free $\mathbb{C}^4$ vacuum}. In its ambient pre-Bang state, the infinite $\mathbb{C}^4$ bulk is subcritical ($g_\Sigma < 1 \implies m_{\text{eff},0}^2 > 0$), possessing neither a $3+1\text{D}$ metric ($\langle\Sigma_{\alpha\beta}\rangle = 0$) nor a causal arrow of time ($\langle H_{\alpha\bar{\beta}}\rangle = 0$), and residing entirely in the time-symmetric Bargmann--Hall--Wightman Extended Tube ($W^+ = W^-$)~\cite{StreaterWightman1964} with zero bare cosmological constant ($\rho_{\text{vac}}(\text{empty }\mathbb{C}^4) = 0$). However, $\mathbb{C}^4$ supports propagating holomorphic spinor ripples $\Psi_\alpha(Z)$. When a large-scale constructive interference (focal caustic) of holomorphic ripples concentrates invariant energy density $\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}(Z)$ above the critical nucleation threshold $\mathcal{E}_{\text{crit}} = (m_{\text{eff},0}^2/\alpha_{\text{E}})^2$, the effective quadratic curvature flips negative, nucleating an expanding $3+1\text{D}$ condensate bubble~\cite{ColemanDeLuccia1980,Volovik2003}. As the bubble expands and cools, it steps through a three-stage symmetry-breaking thermal history: (1) \textbf{Birth of the Arrow of Time} ($\langle H_{\alpha\bar{\beta}}\rangle$ selecting signature $(+,+,-,-)$ and partitioning $\mathbb{PT}^+$ from $\mathbb{PT}^-$~\cite{Penrose1967}); (2) \textbf{Planck/Inflationary Slice Condensation \& Reheating into the Radiation Era} ($\langle\Sigma_{\alpha\beta}\rangle = \rho_{\text{grav}}(t) e^{i\chi} I_{\alpha\beta}$~\cite{PenroseRindler1984}, whose latent heat populates the newly formed $3+1\text{D}$ light-cone surface $r_{\text{Bloch}} = 1$ with massless chiral ripples obeying $w = P/\varepsilon = \frac{1}{3}\langle r_{\text{Bloch}}^2\rangle = 1/3$); and (3) \textbf{Electroweak Higgs Condensation into the Matter Era} ($T_{\text{EW}} \approx 160\text{ GeV}$~\cite{Higgs1964}, tethering counter-propagating radiation ripples into massive Dirac standing waves $E^2 = (mc^2)^2 + (pc)^2$~\cite{Dirac1928,PenroseRoad2004} inside the Bloch ball $r_{\text{Bloch}} = v/c < 1$, driving $w(T) \to 0$). We show how this framework naturally addresses the initial singularity~\cite{PenroseCycles2010}, the origin of the arrow of time, the CMB horizon problem (via pre-light-cone holomorphic equilibration in the $\mathbb{C}^4$ Extended Tube), and the cosmological constant problem (via the expanding bubble's surface-area $\times$ zero-point energy density $\rho_\Lambda = \frac{A_{\text{surface}}}{V_{\text{bulk}}}\frac{\frac{1}{2}\hbar H_0}{\ell_{\text{Pl}}^2} \sim (\hbar H_0 M_{\text{Pl}})^2$). Finally, we contrast the model with standard Twistor Theory and Penrose's Objective Reduction~\cite{PenroseOR1996,DonadiVIP2021}, and derive three falsifiable observational signatures for CMB low-$\ell$ multipoles, primordial gravitational wave parity, and weakening dynamical dark energy ($w_0 > -1, w_a < 0$)~\cite{DESI2024,DESI2025}.

\section{Introduction \& Cosmological Motivation}

Standard Friedmann--Lema\^itre--Robertson--Walker (FLRW) cosmology, combined with General Relativity (GR) and the Standard Model of particle physics, successfully describes the thermal evolution of the universe from a fraction of a second after the Big Bang to the present epoch~\cite{WeinbergCosmology2008}. Yet when extrapolated back to $t \to 0$, the standard $3+1\text{D}$ paradigm confronts four foundational puzzles:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Initial $t = 0$ Singularity Problem:} By the Hawking--Penrose singularity theorems~\cite{HawkingPenrose1970}, evolving a classical $3+1\text{D}$ Lorentzian metric $g_{\mu\nu}$ backward in time under standard energy conditions forces the scale factor $a(t) \to 0$ and the Riemann curvature invariants to diverge ($R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} \to \infty$), signaling the breakdown of $3+1\text{D}$ spacetime geometry itself.
    \item \textbf{The Origin of the Arrow of Time \& Low-Entropy Initial State:} The microscopic laws of QED, QCD, and GR are time-reversal symmetric, yet the universe began with an extraordinarily low gravitational entropy (vanishing Weyl curvature, $C_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma} \to 0$~\cite{PenroseCycles2010}) and a globally directed thermodynamic and causal arrow of time.
    \item \textbf{The Horizon Problem:} Regions of the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) separated by more than $\sim 2^\circ$ on the sky were spacelike-separated (outside each other's $3+1\text{D}$ past light cones) at the time of recombination, yet their temperatures and phase fluctuations are equilibrated to $\Delta T / T \sim 10^{-5}$~\cite{Planck2018}. While scalar-field inflation~\cite{Guth1981,Linde1982} addresses this by postulating an ad-hoc inflaton potential, the microscopic origin of the inflaton and its initial pre-inflationary homogeneity remain open questions.
    \item \textbf{The Cosmological Constant Catastrophe:} If the $3+1\text{D}$ vacuum is treated as a uniform, infinite ground state with Planck-scale zero-point energy density $\rho_{\text{vac}} \sim M_{\text{Pl}}^4$, the resulting cosmological constant exceeds the observed late-time dark energy density $\rho_\Lambda^{\text{obs}} \approx (2.3 \times 10^{-3}\text{ eV})^4$ by $\sim 120$ orders of magnitude~\cite{Weinberg1989,Volovik2003}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Core Thesis of This Paper}
In a two-spinor \textbf{Complexified 4D Vacuum ($\mathbb{C}^4 = \mathbb{C}^2_L \oplus \mathbb{C}^2_R$)}~\cite{Penrose1967,PenroseRindler1984}, $3+1\text{D}$ relativistic kinematics, the Kaluza--Klein $U(1)$ electromagnetic fiber~\cite{Kaluza1921,Klein1926,Wesson1999}, the chiral Dirac equation~\cite{Dirac1928,PenroseRoad2004}, and outside-the-light-cone quantum correlations~\cite{Feynman1949,Bell1964} emerge as low-energy projections of spinor pairing. Crucially, the empty $\mathbb{C}^4$ vacuum need not be unstable on its own: in the subcritical Nambu--Jona-Lasinio (NJL)~\cite{NJL1961} regime, empty $\mathbb{C}^4$ remains uncondensed ($\langle\rho\rangle_{\text{empty }\mathbb{C}^4} = 0$), while localized concentrations of holomorphic 4D ripple energy ($\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}} \ge \mathcal{E}_{\text{crit}}$) nucleate $3+1\text{D}$ condensate droplets.

In this paper, we formulate that mechanism at the cosmological scale: \textbf{we model the Big Bang as the nucleation of an expanding $3+1\text{D}$ condensate bubble inside an infinite, subcritical $\mathbb{C}^4$ vacuum triggered by a massive constructive-interference concentration (focal caustic) of holomorphic 4D ripples.}

\section{The Pre-Bang State: The Infinite Subcritical \texorpdfstring{$\mathbb{C}^4$}{C4} Vacuum}

\subsection{Ambient \texorpdfstring{$SL(4,\mathbb{C})$}{SL(4,C)} Symmetry and Absence of a \texorpdfstring{$3+1\text{D}$}{3+1D} Metric}
Consider an infinite, 4-dimensional complex manifold $\mathbb{C}^4 = \mathbb{C}^2_L \oplus \mathbb{C}^2_R$ parameterized by holomorphic twistor-spinor coordinates~\cite{Penrose1967,PenroseRindler1984,Newman1976}:
\begin{equation}
Z^\alpha = \big(\omega^A,\, \pi_{A'}\big) \in \mathbb{C}^4 \qquad (\alpha = 0,1,2,3;\quad A = 0,1;\quad A' = 0',1').
\end{equation}
Prior to spontaneous symmetry breaking, the ambient symmetry group of $\mathbb{C}^4$ is the complex special linear group $SL(4,\mathbb{C})$, and the microscopic dynamics is governed purely by massless twistor-spinor fields $\Psi_\alpha(Z) = (\xi_A(Z), \eta^{A'}(Z))$ with four-spinor contact interactions~\cite{NJL1961}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{micro}}(\Psi) = i\hbar c\, \bar{\Psi}_{\bar{\alpha}} \mathcal{D}^{\bar{\alpha}\beta} \Psi_\beta + \frac{G_H}{2}\left(\bar{\Psi}_{\bar{\alpha}}\Psi_\beta\right)\left(\bar{\Psi}_{\bar{\beta}}\Psi_\alpha\right) + \frac{G_\Sigma}{4}\left| \Psi_{[\alpha}\tilde{\Psi}_{\beta]} \right|^2 + G_{\text{EW}}\left(\xi^A \eta_A\right)\left(\bar{\xi}_{A'}\bar{\eta}^{A'}\right).
\end{equation}
Introducing the collective sesquilinear slice-order parameter $H_{\alpha\bar{\beta}} \sim \langle \Psi_\alpha \bar{\Psi}_{\bar{\beta}}\rangle$ and the antisymmetric bi-twistor condensate $\Sigma_{\alpha\beta} = \rho\, e^{i\chi} I_{\alpha\beta} \sim \langle \Psi_{[\alpha}\tilde{\Psi}_{\beta]}\rangle$ via Hubbard--Stratonovich auxiliary fields and integrating out 1-loop spinor fluctuations up to the cutoff $\Lambda_{\text{UV}}$ yields the empty-vacuum quadratic stiffness~\cite{NJL1961,ColemanWeinberg1973}:
\begin{equation}
m_{\text{eff},0}^2 \equiv \frac{1}{G_\Sigma} - \frac{N_f g_{\text{Y}}^2 \Lambda_{\text{UV}}^2}{8\pi^2} = \frac{1 - g_\Sigma}{G_\Sigma}, \qquad g_\Sigma \equiv \frac{N_f g_{\text{Y}}^2 \Lambda_{\text{UV}}^2}{8\pi^2}\, G_\Sigma.
\end{equation}

Our foundational cosmological postulate is that the infinite ambient $\mathbb{C}^4$ vacuum lies in the \textbf{subcritical regime}:
\begin{equation}
g_\Sigma < 1 \quad \implies \quad m_{\text{eff},0}^2 > 0 \quad \implies \quad \langle H_{\alpha\bar{\beta}} \rangle_{\text{bulk}} = 0, \quad \langle \Sigma_{\alpha\beta} \rangle_{\text{bulk}} = 0.
\end{equation}
This subcritical pre-Bang vacuum exhibits three remarkable physical properties:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Primordial Spinor Vacuum Ontology (Gravity Does Not Pre-Exist the Condensation):} In the ambient $\mathbb{C}^4$ bulk, the \emph{only} fundamental pre-existing quantum field is the primordial spinor field $\Psi_\alpha(Z)$. The auxiliary field $\Sigma_{\alpha\beta}(Z) \equiv \bar{\Psi}_{[\alpha}(Z)\Psi_{\beta]}(Z)$ is merely a Hubbard--Stratonovich collective shorthand for spinor pairing (analogous to Cooper pairs in BCS superconductivity). Because $\langle \Sigma_{\alpha\beta} \rangle_{\text{bulk}} = 0$, the gravitational field and the $3+1\text{D}$ metric $ds_{4\text{D}}^2 \propto |\langle\Sigma_{\alpha\beta}\rangle Z_1^\alpha Z_2^\beta|^{-2}$ do not exist prior to condensation; empty $\mathbb{C}^4$ has no rulers, no clocks, and no preferred real $(3,1)$ slice. Gravity is born at the condensation event itself as the collective elastic Goldstone/hydrodynamic mode of the phase-locked spinors.
    \item \textbf{Time-Symmetric Extended Tube (No Arrow of Time):} Because $\langle H_{\alpha\bar{\beta}} \rangle_{\text{bulk}} = 0$, the positive-frequency forward tube $\mathbb{PT}^+$ and negative-frequency backward tube $\mathbb{PT}^-$ are not yet separated. The entire ambient $\mathbb{C}^4$ bulk exists in the time-symmetric Bargmann--Hall--Wightman \textbf{Extended Tube}~\cite{StreaterWightman1964}, where forward and backward correlators are identical ($W^+(Z_1, Z_2) = W^-(Z_1, Z_2)$).
    \item \textbf{Zero Bare Cosmological Constant in the Bulk:} Because the order parameters vanish identically in the uncondensed $\mathbb{C}^4$ bulk ($\langle\rho\rangle_{\text{bulk}} = 0$), the infinite ambient $\mathbb{C}^4$ space carries zero condensate potential energy:
    \begin{equation}
    \rho_{\text{vac}}\big(\text{empty }\mathbb{C}^4\text{ bulk}\big) = V_{\text{eff}}\big(\rho = 0;\, \mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}} = 0\big) = 0.
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Holomorphic Spinor Ripples in Pre-Bang \texorpdfstring{$\mathbb{C}^4$}{C4}}
Although the subcritical $\mathbb{C}^4$ bulk has no $3+1\text{D}$ metric or rest-mass scale, it supports massless holomorphic spinor wavepackets (``4D ripples'') satisfying the Cauchy--Riemann holomorphy condition $\bar{\partial}_{\bar{\alpha}}\Psi_\beta(Z) = 0$:

\section{The Nucleation Mechanism: Constructive-Interference Caustics \& Bubble Formation in \texorpdfstring{$\mathbb{C}^4$}{C4}}

\subsection{Energy-Triggered Instability from a 4D Ripple Caustic}
In an infinite wave-supporting manifold $\mathbb{C}^4$, linear superpositions of holomorphic wavepackets $\Psi_\alpha(Z)$ inevitably form focal regions or \textbf{constructive-interference caustics} where intersecting left-handed ($\xi_A$) and right-handed ($\eta^{A'}$) ripples overlap coherently. Within such a focal region $\Omega_{\text{Bang}} \subset \mathbb{C}^4$, the local invariant ripple energy density $\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}(Z)$ rises sharply.

Coupling the background ripple density $\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}(Z)$ to the collective condensate modulus $\rho(Z)$ via the Yukawa interaction $-\frac{g_{\text{Y}}}{2}(\Psi^\alpha\Sigma_{\alpha\beta}\Psi^\beta + \text{h.c.})$ shifts the local effective potential by $-\frac{1}{2}\alpha_{\text{E}}\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}^{1/2}(Z)\rho^2(Z)$~\cite{NJL1961,ColemanWeinberg1973}:
\begin{equation}
V_{\text{eff}}\big(\rho;\, \mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}(Z)\big) = \frac{1}{2}\Big( m_{\text{eff},0}^2 - \alpha_{\text{E}}\, \mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}^{1/2}(Z) \Big)\rho^2 + \frac{\lambda_{\text{eff}}}{4}\rho^4 - \epsilon_{\text{cubic}}\, \rho^3,
\end{equation}
where $\epsilon_{\text{cubic}}$ accounts for the determinant coupling $\det(H)\,\varepsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}\Sigma_{\alpha\beta}\Sigma_{\gamma\delta}$ between Stage~1 and Stage~2 order parameters.

Whenever a constructive-interference caustic in $\mathbb{C}^4$ concentrates ripple energy density above the \textbf{critical Big Bang nucleation threshold}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}(Z) \ge \mathcal{E}_{\text{crit}} \equiv \left( \frac{m_{\text{eff},0}^2}{\alpha_{\text{E}}} \right)^2 = \left( \frac{1 - g_\Sigma}{\alpha_{\text{E}} G_\Sigma} \right)^2 \sim M_{\text{Pl}}^4,
\end{equation}
the effective quadratic stiffness flips sign ($m_{\text{eff}}^2(\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}) \equiv m_{\text{eff},0}^2 - \alpha_{\text{E}}\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}^{1/2} < 0$), creating a deep symmetry-breaking minimum at:
\begin{equation}
\rho_{\text{Bang}}\big(\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}\big) = \sqrt{\frac{\alpha_{\text{E}}\, \mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}}^{1/2} - m_{\text{eff},0}^2}{\lambda_{\text{eff}}}} \sim M_{\text{Pl}} > 0.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Coleman--De Luccia Nucleation of the \texorpdfstring{$3+1\text{D}$}{3+1D} Bubble}
Consider a localized 4D caustic region of radius $R$ inside $\mathbb{C}^4$ where $\mathcal{E}_{\text{loc}} > \mathcal{E}_{\text{crit}}$. The free-energy difference (Euclidean bounce action $S_E$~\cite{ColemanDeLuccia1980}) between the uncondensed $\mathbb{C}^4$ phase ($\rho = 0$) and a nucleated $3+1\text{D}$ condensate droplet ($\rho = \rho_{\text{Bang}}$) of radius $R$ is governed by the competition between the negative bulk condensation energy $-\Delta V_{\text{eff}} = \frac{1}{4}\lambda_{\text{eff}}\rho_{\text{Bang}}^4 > 0$ and the positive surface tension $\sigma_{\text{wall}} = \int_0^{\rho_{\text{Bang}}} d\rho\, \sqrt{2 V_{\text{eff}}(\rho)}$ of the $\mathbb{C}^4 \to 3+1\text{D}$ phase boundary where $\nabla_\perp \Psi_{\text{grav}} \neq 0$:
\begin{equation}
S_E(R) = 2\pi^2 R^3 \sigma_{\text{wall}} - \frac{\pi^2}{2} R^4 \Delta V_{\text{eff}}.
\end{equation}
Extremizing $S_E(R)$ with respect to $R$ ($\partial S_E / \partial R = 0$) yields the \textbf{critical Big Bang nucleation radius} $R_{\text{crit}}$:
\begin{equation}
R_{\text{crit}} = \frac{3\sigma_{\text{wall}}}{\Delta V_{\text{eff}}} \sim \frac{M_{\text{Pl}}}{\sqrt{\Delta V_{\text{eff}}}} > 0, \qquad S_E(R_{\text{crit}}) = \frac{27\pi^2 \sigma_{\text{wall}}^4}{2(\Delta V_{\text{eff}})^3}.
\end{equation}

This yields our first major cosmological result:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Elimination of the $t = 0$ Point Singularity:} A $3+1\text{D}$ spacetime bubble \emph{cannot} nucleate at zero radius ($R = 0$); it is born at a finite minimum conformal radius $R_{\text{crit}} > 0$ determined by the $\mathbb{C}^4 \to 3+1\text{D}$ interface tension $\sigma_{\text{wall}}$. If one mathematically extrapolates an FLRW universe backward to energy densities $\mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{E}_{\text{crit}}$, the metric does not crush into a singular point $a(0) = 0$; instead, the metric order parameter $\langle\Sigma_{\alpha\beta}\rangle$ \textbf{melts smoothly back into the scale-free conformal $\mathbb{C}^4$ phase}.
    \item \textbf{Microscopic Origin of Penrose's Weyl Curvature Hypothesis ($C_{ABCD}(t=0) = 0$):} Because the pre-Bang $\mathbb{C}^4$ state is conformally flat ($SL(4,\mathbb{C})$-symmetric) and the nucleating condensate $\langle\Sigma_{\alpha\beta}\rangle = \rho_{\text{Bang}} e^{i\chi} I_{\alpha\beta}$ is proportional to the isotropic Infinity Twistor $I_{\alpha\beta}$, the initial Weyl spinor of the nucleated $3+1\text{D}$ bubble vanishes identically at nucleation ($C_{ABCD}(t_{\text{nuc}}) = 0$), explaining the extraordinarily low gravitational entropy of the early universe~\cite{PenroseCycles2010}.
\end{itemize}

This naturally generates both **primordial inflation** and **reheating** without introducing an ad-hoc inflaton scalar:
