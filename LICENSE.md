# Licensing (decision pending)

This is a demonstration repository. A real journal must settle licensing deliberately, because two different kinds of material live here and they want different licenses:

- **Articles, reviews, assessments, tutors (content):** recommended **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY-4.0)** — open access with attribution, the norm for open scholarship.
- **Any tooling / scripts (code):** recommended a permissive software license such as **MIT** or **Apache-2.0**.

A subtle, unsettled question this journal raises directly: **how is AI-authored content licensed, and who holds the rights?** The honest-byline policy (an AI listed as a contributor or co-author) interacts with copyright law that, in most jurisdictions as of 2026, does not recognize non-human authors. Until resolved, the safe interpretation is that the **human author/maintainer holds and licenses the rights**, with the AI's contribution disclosed in `provenance.yaml`.

**Action required before this goes public:** replace this file with the chosen license text(s).
