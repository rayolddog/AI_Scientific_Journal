**Dispositions:** *Accepted* (the critique holds → linked to the correction that implements it) · *Contested* (the authors disagree, and say why → escalates to the §5 resolution loop if a reviewer maintains it) · *Deferred* (acknowledged but out of scope here → a recorded limitation).

> The response is the hinge between the reviews and everything that follows. An **unanswered** critique is treated as *unresolved*, never as conceded.
