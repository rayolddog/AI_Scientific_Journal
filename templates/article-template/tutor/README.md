# Tutor

The **recursive tutor** (manifesto Pillar IV): a layered explainer that meets the reader at their level *and recurses into the cited references*, so a reader can follow the chain of support rather than taking citations on faith.

- [`index.md`](index.md) — entry point. At minimum, provide:
  1. **Three reading levels** — e.g. *for a non-specialist*, *for an adjacent-field scientist*, *for a specialist* (skip to the contested step).
  2. **Key references explained** — for each load-bearing citation, a short plain-language account of what that source actually shows and why this paper leans on it. This is also the citation-integrity check: a reference that cannot be explained was probably misused.

In a live journal this is an interactive agent; in this demonstration it is written Markdown.
